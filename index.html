Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Protodex is a fan-made index of Protogen characters and their owners. This policy explains what information the site handles and where it goes. In short: Protodex runs entirely in your browser and does not send your data to us.

1. Who we are

Protodex (“Protodex”, “we”, “us”) is the operator of this website. For any question or request about this policy, contact us at patch@protodex.dev.

2. What we collect

Information you enter. When you add or import a Protogen, the details you provide — character name, owner, Instagram and Telegram handles, country, profile link, notes and any photo — are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. We do not receive, transmit or store this information on a server.

Photos. Uploaded images are resized and compressed in your browser, then kept with your saved Protogens. Images added by URL are loaded directly from the third-party site that hosts them.

Admin password. The admin password is stored in this page only as a salted, hashed value. The password itself is never saved.

Technical data. The page uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. If you open Protodex through a hosting provider or web server, that provider may automatically log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your request under its own policies. We do not control or use those logs.

3. Cookies and local storage

Protodex does not set cookies. It uses local storage and session storage on your device for the features described in the Cookie Policy.

4. Why we handle this information

Because this data stays on your device, we do not process it for our own purposes. Where data protection law (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR) applies, our handling is limited to:

  • storing the content you choose to add so the index works as you expect;
  • protecting the admin view with a password; and
  • replying to you if you contact us by email.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. If you follow a link to Instagram, Telegram, Fur Affinity or another service, that service may collect information about you under its own privacy policy. Any photo or profile link you add from another service is loaded from that service, which will see your IP address when it is requested.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved Protogens remain in your browser until you remove them in the admin view or clear site data in your browser settings. Because we never receive this data, we cannot delete it for you — clearing it on your device removes it completely. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Since the index data lives only on your device, you can exercise most of these directly: export it with “Export JSON”, or delete it using your browser’s clear-site-data option. For anything else, email patch@protodex.dev.

8. Security

Protodex is a static page with no server of its own. Data in local storage is not encrypted and can be read by anyone with access to your browser profile or device. The admin password is a deterrent to casual visitors, not a guarantee of security. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in the details you add.

9. Children

Protodex is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under the age of majority where you live, please use the site with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact Privacy questions and data requests: patch@protodex.dev

Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Protodex uses cookies and similar browser storage. The short version: no cookies and no tracking — only the local storage the index needs to work.

1. Cookies

Protodex does not set, read or use cookies. There are no analytics cookies, advertising cookies, social media cookies or preference cookies.

2. Local storage we use

The site uses two browser storage mechanisms that are technically similar to cookies but are never sent to a server:

  • localStorage — protodex.protogens.v1. Stores the Protogens you add, including any photos, so the index survives a reload. It stays until you delete it.
  • sessionStorage — protodex.admin.session. Remembers that you unlocked the admin view in this tab. It is cleared when you close the tab or press “Lock”.

Both are strictly necessary for the site to work as described. They contain no identifiers produced by us and are not shared with anyone.

3. Third-party content

Protodex does not embed third-party cookies or tracking scripts. If a Protogen links to, or loads an image from, another service (for example Instagram, Telegram or Fur Affinity), your browser contacts that service directly and its cookie and privacy policies apply. We have no control over those cookies.

4. Hosting

If you access Protodex through a hosting provider, that provider may set its own cookies or log technical information for security and delivery. Those cookies are controlled by the provider, not by Protodex; check the provider’s cookie policy.

5. Managing storage

You can clear Protodex’s local data by deleting site data for this page in your browser settings. Blocking local storage will stop new Protogens from being saved. Browser help pages explain how to view and delete cookies and site data.

Contact Questions about cookies or storage: patch@protodex.dev
